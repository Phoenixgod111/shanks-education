# Shanks Math content policy

## Scope

Shanks teaches canonical mathematical concepts in its own words. A textbook is
used only to help a student locate the same concept in the order used at school.

## Allowed

- Original explanations, examples, exercises, diagrams, and answer feedback.
- Neutral names of mathematical concepts.
- Bibliographic identification needed to select a textbook line.
- Independently created mappings from textbook sections to canonical Shanks topics.
- Public curriculum requirements and mathematical facts.
- Original Shanks cover tiles built from typography, neutral geometry, and
  mathematical symbols that do not imitate a publisher's cover.

## Not allowed without a recorded licence

- Copied textbook paragraphs, exercises, illustrations, scans, or cover artwork.
- Filtered, recolored, traced, AI-restyled, or otherwise modified versions of
  real cover artwork.
- Publisher logos or a layout likely to be mistaken for an official edition.
- Invented ISBNs, editions, page numbers, paragraph numbers, or quotations.
- Content whose origin, review status, or permission cannot be explained.

## Publication states

- `generated`: produced but not eligible for students.
- `auto_checked`: schema and deterministic checks passed; shown as AI beta.
- `reviewed`: checked by a named mathematics reviewer.
- `published`: eligible for the selected release channel.

Automatic checks do not constitute pedagogical review. Until a lesson has
`reviewed` status, the interface must display “AI-бета” and provide a report
action.

## Corrections

Reports about a lesson must retain the lesson ID, version, step ID, category,
and timestamp. A corrected lesson receives a new content version; existing
progress remains attached to its canonical topic and stable step aliases.

## References

The textbook catalog must record the source used to verify each line and the
date of verification. For the 2026–2027 release, the authoritative baseline is
the Russian federal list approved by Ministry of Education order No. 436 dated
22 June 2026. Product metadata must still be checked against the official list
before a textbook route is marked reviewed.
